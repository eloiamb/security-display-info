Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Security Display is a personal app used only by its owner to view the owner's own Google Nest cameras.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (through Google's Smart Device Management API), the app reads the list of the owner's cameras and their live video streams.

What it stores

The only thing stored is a Google sign-in key, kept in a file on the owner's own computer. Video is shown live and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded anywhere by this app.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or sent to anyone. The app has no servers of its own; it communicates only with Google.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google account's security settings or in the Google Home app.
